# Glossary

## Forecasting terms
QLIKE is the quasi-likelihood loss used to score variance forecasts. MSE is mean squared error. DM stands for the Diebold-Mariano test of equal predictive accuracy. HAC means heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation consistent standard errors, here Newey-West.

## Model names
GARCH is Generalised Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity. GJR is the Glosten-Jagannathan-Runkle asymmetric extension. EWMA is the exponentially weighted moving average. HAR is the Heterogeneous AutoRegressive model. LightGBM is a gradient boosting library.

## Risk terms
VaR is Value-at-Risk and ES is Expected Shortfall. FHS is filtered historical simulation. POF is the Kupiec proportion-of-failures test. CC is conditional coverage, the combination of coverage and independence tests. A breach, violation or exception all mean a loss larger than the VaR.

## Data terms
OHLCV means open, high, low, close and volume. OOS means out-of-sample, the period after the first training window. A ticker is the exchange symbol of a security, for example RELIANCE or TCS.
